# Letscool Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Letscool Aircon Engineering Pte. Ltd. provides aircon sales, installation, servicing, repairs, and maintenance for residential and commercial customers across Singapore. The proposed homepage helps visitors choose a service, understand the full cost, and send an enquiry.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make services easier to find.** Services and promotions compete for attention on the original homepage. A service-focused hero and six consistently styled cards would help visitors distinguish routine servicing from specialist cleaning and repairs, with a direct enquiry link on each card.
2. **Clarify pricing and make company credentials easier to find.** Service rates, transport fees, and package conditions can be difficult to compare. Pricing tables would show the applicable fees and conditions beside each rate. A dedicated section would bring together the company’s stated experience, customer reviews, and applicable workmanship warranty terms.
3. **Shorten the mobile enquiry path.** Visitors currently have to look through multiple sections for contact options. A persistent mobile bar with Call and WhatsApp buttons, enquiry links beside services and prices, and a form with service selection would make it easier to get in touch.

## Design Improvements

Clear headings and concise descriptions would separate promotions from service choices. Consistent card layouts and aligned pricing tables would make services, fees, and enquiry options easier to scan.
